Examples

What will it cost me?

Pick the billing method that matches how often each QR code is scanned. Examples below use €0.05 per credit (10,000-credit pack) and a 30-day month.

Daily check-in point

A check-in QR code scanned at arrival and departure, about 2 scans a day. Pay per day: 30 credits a month, about €1.50. Pay per scan would cost 60 credits.

Occasional feedback point

A customer feedback QR code scanned about 10 times a month. Pay per scan: 10 credits a month, about €0.50, and nothing in quiet months.

The simple rule

Fewer than one scan a day on average? Choose pay per scan. More than one a day? Choose pay per day, which covers up to 50 scans a day for 1 credit.

FAQ

Any questions? Answered

How much does a credit cost?

Credits are sold in packs in the online store of the platform. For example, with a pack of 10,000 credits, one credit costs €0.05.

What happens to my QR codes if I run out of credits?

Each QR-code tracker needs 1 credit per day or per scan to work. With no credits left, your QR codes stop working until you top up your account.

What if I choose daily billing and go over 50 scans?

You're charged 1 additional credit for each extra pack of 50 scans during the same day.

Which billing method should I choose?

Choose pay per scan for QR codes scanned less than once a day on average, such as feedback points: you pay nothing when they are not used. Choose pay per day for QR codes scanned several times a day, such as check-in points: 1 credit covers up to 50 scans.

Can I use different billing methods for different QR codes?

Yes. You choose the billing method for each QR-code tracker in its settings, so you can mix both across your sites.

Are users, workers or connections limited?

No. Both billing methods include unlimited users, unlimited workers and unlimited software connections, plus API access, the app generator and online support.

How do I try Ubiqod for free?

Create an account: 100 free credits are added so you can create and test your first QR codes before buying a credit pack.
