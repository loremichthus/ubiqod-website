FAQ

Any questions? Answered

How much does a credit cost?

Credits are sold in packs in the online store of the platform. For example, with a pack of 10,000 credits, one credit costs €0.05.

What happens to my QR codes if I run out of credits?

Each QR-code tracker needs 1 credit per day or per scan to work. With no credits left, your QR codes stop working until you top up your account.

What if I choose daily billing and go over 50 scans?

You're charged 1 additional credit for each extra pack of 50 scans during the same day.
